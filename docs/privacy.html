Data Use / Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026. This policy applies to the independent Rogues Gallery Android app, which is unaffiliated with League of Comic Geeks (LCG).

Signing in and reading your collection

You sign in as yourself on League's own HTTPS website in an embedded browser. Your password is submitted directly to League. Rogues Gallery does not read, retain, or share your LCG password with its developer or any third parties. It only reads your Have It collection to display covers; it does not change your League collection.

Information kept on your device

The app caches downloaded cover images in private device storage, bounded to 256 MiB and 1000 cached files. It also saves your display settings and references to comics you exclude, separated by account. If you enable selection persistence, it saves references to your selected comics so it can choose them again. Collection titles, image addresses, and the full collection list are held in memory while the app runs, rather than saved as a collection database.

League's embedded browser retains session cookies and website storage so you can remain signed in. These belong to the League browser session; the app does not inspect them to obtain your password. Android backup is disabled for the app. Cached covers may be removed by Android at any time.

Network requests and sharing

Collection requests go directly to League, and cover requests go to the image servers supplied by League. Those services receive normal connection information, such as your IP address and requested image. Rogues Gallery has no developer-operated server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or collection upload service. It does not send your credentials, saved selections, exclusions, or app activity to its developer or independent third parties.

League's website and any services it embeds operate under their own policies. This app's policy does not govern League or its image providers. Review League's policies on its website: League of Comic Geeks.

Your controls

Use Settings to clear cached covers. Use Manage collection to check or uncheck covers; excluded references stay on your device until you re-enable those covers or clear the app's data. Turning off selection persistence deletes saved selection references. Sign out clears League session cookies and website storage and discards the current in-memory collection; your local settings, exclusions, and cached covers remain. Clear the app's storage through Android Settings or uninstall it to remove all its local data.

Questions

For questions about the app or this policy, contact the project maintainer through the Rogues Gallery repository. Do not include passwords or session cookies.
