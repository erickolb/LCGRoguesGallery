LCG Rogues Gallery

Your comic collection, out of the boxes and onto the screen.

An experimental Android fullscreen cover gallery for your League of Comic Geeks Have It collection. Includes manual browsing and a slideshow.

Live integration is awaiting verification with a signed-in account. This is an unofficial app.
